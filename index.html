कलम दवात पूजा

Kalam Dawat Puja

In worship of Shri Chitragupt Maharaj Ji, who holds the pen and keeps the record.

Step through
लेखकThe Scribe

He who keeps the account

Tradition holds that Shri Chitragupt Maharaj Ji came forth from the body of Brahma after a long meditation, already holding a pen and an inkpot. He sits beside Yama and writes down what each person does.

The Kayastha community takes its name from that origin: kaya, the body.

ॐ श्री चित्रगुप्ताय नमः

On Dwitiya, the pens rest

Two days after Diwali, the pen and the inkpot are placed before him and worshipped. In many families no pen is lifted until the puja is done. The first words written afterwards are his name.

लेखनThe Writing
The pen is dipped. The first line is for Shri Ganesha.

Puja mode keeps this desk on screen and the pen writing, for as long as the lamp is lit.

विद्याThe Offering of Study

Books kept open before the lamp

Whatever you learn from belongs in this puja. The notebooks, the textbooks, the laptop this page is open on: today they sit beside the inkpot.

Hindi or English. The pen will write it in the notebook.
